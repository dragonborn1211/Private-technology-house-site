Frequently asked questions

Briefly about price, timelines and what stays with you.

Apps

How much does an app cost?

From 150,000 ₽ for one platform. A second platform is +50,000 ₽, a desktop version is +100,000 ₽. We give the exact estimate after a call, before we start.

Which to choose: an app or a Telegram Mini App?

A Mini App opens right inside Telegram with nothing to install: handy for catalogs, bookings and orders. A separate app is for when you need an icon on the phone and a listing in the stores. We’ll advise on a call.

Will you publish the app in the stores?

Yes: Google Play, RuStore and the App Store — build, store listing, updates. The developer accounts are registered in your name.

How can we see the app before launch?

You install a test build on your phone and try it live, before publishing.

Can the app be connected to our CRM?

Yes: requests and orders from the app go straight to Bitrix24 and Telegram.
