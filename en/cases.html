Game website · interactive · RU / EN

Resonara: Soulstrings

A website for a game that is still in development. There is no trailer yet, so the website itself carries the idea of the game: you can touch it and hear it.

Open the website ↗Run your cursor over the strings and turn on the sound, top right.Touch the strings and turn on the sound, top right.

Three highlights

  1. 01

    Living strings

    The whole background is taut strings. Run your cursor or finger over them and they quiver like real ones. The game is called Soulstrings, and you get it in the first second, without a single word.

  2. 02

    Your choice changes the site

    The visitor decides whom to trust: Miri, the path of strength, or Piri, the path of mercy. The page instantly takes on the hero’s color. The game itself works the same way: every decision changes the world.

  3. 03

    The strings sound

    Turn on the sound and every string answers with its own note. Nothing is pre-recorded: the site plucks the string right in the browser. So it sounds alive, and the site stays light.

  • Design
  • String animation
  • Sound
  • Hero choice
  • Location map

Observatory site · booking · EN

Antares — observatory

A website for a private observatory with a guesthouse in the mountains. Its job is to sell a night under the stars to someone who has never looked through a big telescope. The background is a long-exposure starry sky that slowly turns as you scroll.

Open the website ↗Press T to see tonight’s sky.The switch at the top shows tonight’s sky.

Three highlights

  1. 01

    How the telescope works

    A layered diagram shows the path of light: from the guest’s eye through the eyepiece, mirror and dome all the way to the star. The telescope window shows what you can see: Saturn, star clusters, nebulae. The hard part is clear without a lecture.

  2. 02

    Tonight’s sky

    The site works out the Moon phase, the next new moon and the darkest nights on its own. Guests see when it’s best to come, and the owner doesn’t have to update anything by hand.

  3. 03

    Booking in one sentence

    Night packages with prices and a form where guests write their wishes in their own words. Before sending, they see what their request will look like.

  • Sky animation
  • Moon calculations
  • Packages and prices

AI · Bitrix24

Review every call

AI listens to sales calls and tells each manager what to do better.

How it works

Before

Lots of calls and just one head of sales. There was no way to listen to them all, so only some got checked. Managers rarely heard about their mistakes, and usually only after the client was gone.

What we built

Every conversation is recorded and turned into text. AI checks it against the rules of a good call: did the manager say hello, find out what the client needs, handle objections, agree on the next step. The review with a score appears right in the client’s CRM card. A daily summary can come to Telegram, handy for those who have no time to open the CRM.

What changed

  • Feedback on every call, not just a few.
  • Managers see their own mistakes and get better, and sales grow with them.
  • The head of sales no longer spends hours listening to calls.
  • Client names and phone numbers are removed before analysis, so personal data never goes to the AI.

AI · Telegram bot · dashboard

From meeting to finished work

A system for a team of artists: tasks appear on their own after each meeting, and a Telegram bot guides every one through to delivery.

How it works

Before

Tasks were set out loud on calls, and they kept getting lost. Not everyone remembered who was doing what and by when, and the lead handed out tasks and chased deadlines by hand.

What we built

Calls are recorded, and any service with recording will do: Discord, Yandex Telemost and others. AI transcribes the conversation and picks out decisions, tasks, owners and deadlines. The lead gets the list in Telegram and confirms it with one button, or fixes it by hand if AI got something wrong. The meeting summary goes to the team group. From there the bot runs the tasks: each one has a card, and the bot takes in work, passes on edits and reminds people about deadlines.

What changed

  • Not a single task from a meeting gets lost anymore.
  • Artists get tasks, edits and reminders in one place: Telegram.
  • The lead has a dashboard: the plan by week and progress in percent, overdue tasks, who is overloaded and how many person-days are left.
  • The same dashboard collects stats from the YouTube and Telegram channels.
  • And all of it is cheap to run.

AI · SEO analytics · Telegram

SEO report arrives on its own in Telegram

Once a week a summary arrives in Telegram showing how the site is doing in search, with conclusions and a plan for the week. The full report opens via a link as a clear page with charts.

How it works

Before

To see how the site is doing in search, you had to open Metrica, Webmaster, a rank tracker and PageSpeed, put the numbers together and work out what they mean. A manager won’t do that, and a manual report takes hours.

What we built

On a schedule, a bot collects the data itself: visits and bounces from Metrica, index status and errors from Webmaster, search rankings, site speed on mobile and desktop, and website leads from the CRM. AI compares it with the previous week and writes conclusions in plain language and a plan: what is urgent, what is important, what to schedule. A short summary arrives in Telegram, and the full report with charts and tips opens via a link.

What the report looks like

Example with test data.

What changed

  • In a minute you can see whether the site is growing or slipping.
  • You can see which queries are about to reach the top 10, and they get worked on first.
  • Traffic and leads in one report: it’s clear whether search brings in customers.
  • The weekly work plan is ready without a dedicated analyst.
  • Any service with an open API can be connected, on any schedule.

Automation · AI

A catalog that fills itself

Products appear on the website with specs and an SEO description, with no manual entry.

How it works

Before

Every product was added to the website by hand: find the specs, gather them in one place, copy them into the product page one by one, write a description. When a product has lots of specs, a single page takes ages.

What we built

Product data is uploaded as one file from wherever is convenient: a price list, a spreadsheet, a supplier export. The system creates the product pages and fills in the specs on its own, and AI writes an SEO description for each product. Names and specs come only from the data, AI doesn’t make them up, so there are no mistakes in them. You can publish right away or after a check. When everything is ready, a report arrives in Telegram.

What changed

  • New products appear on the website with no manual work.
  • Every product has its own SEO description, so it is easier to find in search.
  • Everything except the description comes from the data as is.
  • A report in Telegram: what was published and when.

AI · knowledge base

AI assistant for every department

An employee asks a work question, and the assistant answers from company documents.

How it works

Before

Answers to work questions lived in policies, manuals and the heads of experienced staff. To find something out, people had to dig through folders or distract colleagues.

What we built

Each department (sales, accounting, support) has its own assistant and its own knowledge base. You can ask in Telegram or in a web chat. The assistant looks for the answer in documents the staff uploaded themselves, so it answers by the company’s rules, not “from the internet”. The bases can be split by department or shared, whichever suits the company.

What changed

  • An answer to a work question in seconds, at any time.
  • Departments add to the base themselves, so it doesn’t go out of date.
  • Each department sees only its own documents.
  • The knowledge base is kept on our server.

Integration · Bitrix24

Orders straight to CRM and Telegram

An order from the website, the Telegram mini app or the mobile app goes straight to the CRM, and the manager hears about it in Telegram.

How it works

Before

Customers order wherever it suits them: on the website, in Telegram, in the app. When orders land in different places, they are easy to miss, and the manager hears about them late.

What we built

The catalog, cart and checkout work on the website, in the Telegram mini app and in the phone app. Every order creates the customer and a request in Bitrix24 on its own, together with the cart contents. The notification goes to whoever needs it: a manager or the team group.

What changed

  • All orders in one CRM, wherever they come from.
  • The manager hears about an order right away.
  • The cart contents are already in the card, so there’s no need to ask the customer again.

Avito, Wildberries and Ozon connect the same way: they offer official access for software (an API).
