Frequently asked questions

Briefly about price, timelines and what stays with you.

Integrations

How much does a Bitrix24 integration cost?

From 5,000 ₽ a month per scenario: launch and maintenance. The exact price depends on the scenario and volume; we’ll name it after a short call.

What do you need to connect our Bitrix24?

A Bitrix24 webhook with the right permissions and access to the services we connect: the form on your website, a Telegram bot, a marketplace account. We set up the rest ourselves.

Do we need to install anything in Bitrix24?

Usually not: we connect through a webhook. The scenario runs on our server, not in your portal.

How does call review work?

Recordings are turned into text and a neural network reviews the conversation: did the manager find out what the client needs, how did they handle objections, did they agree on the next step. The review goes into the client card, the daily summary into Telegram. Client names and phone numbers are removed before analysis.

Can Bitrix24 be connected to Avito or marketplaces?

Yes, if the service offers official access for software: that’s how Avito, Wildberries and Ozon connect. Orders and inquiries land in Bitrix24 along with everything else.
