Project · one-off

Websites and apps

  1. 01

    Call and estimate

    We go through the task and examples you like. Scope, timeline and price are named before we start.

  2. 02

    Concept

    We show the idea: the first screen of the site or the key screens of the app. Agreed before the build.

  3. 03

    Prototype

    You open the site at a temporary address and install the app as a test build on your phone.

  4. 04

    Edits

    We apply your comments. The number of revision rounds is fixed in the estimate.

  5. 05

    Launch

    The site moves to your domain, the app is published to Google Play, RuStore and the App Store.

What we need from you: access to the domain, texts and photos if you have them. For an app, developer accounts in the stores.

Project · per task

Servers

  1. 01

    Review

    We look at what you have: the server, website or service and what is wrong with it.

  2. 02

    Access

    You give us access to the server, hosting and domain.

  3. 03

    Setup

    Docker, domains and SSL, scheduled backups, monitoring. In an outage we find the cause first.

  4. 04

    Testing

    We make sure a backup restores and an outage alert arrives.

  5. 05

    Handover

    We hand over a description of what is set up, where and how. The access stays with you.

What we need from you: access to the server, hosting and domain.

Subscription · monthly

Website care

  1. 01

    Migration

    We move the site to our hosting. Russian projects stay on servers in Russia.

  2. 02

    Monitoring and backups

    We set up uptime checks and scheduled backups. We learn about an outage before you do.

  3. 03

    152-FZ

    Privacy policy, form consents and a cookie banner.

  4. 04

    Edits

    You tell us in Telegram what to change: texts, photos, prices. The amount of edits is set in the plan.

  5. 05

    Every month

    We renew the domain and SSL and update the site. The payment is fixed.

What we need from you: access to your current hosting and domain.

Subscription · setup + monthly

Automation and AI

  1. 01

    The task

    You tell us what is done by hand or who the agent should answer. We decide what goes to the machine and what stays with people.

  2. 02

    Access and documents

    A Bitrix24 webhook, a Telegram bot, API keys. For an agent: policies, price lists, frequent questions.

  3. 03

    Build

    We build the workflow or the agent on our server: n8n, Cloudflare Workers or our own code.

  4. 04

    Testing

    We run it on real data and questions, show the result and fix what is needed.

  5. 05

    Looked after

    We watch it work, fix it when an API changes and update the knowledge base.

What we need from you: access to the services; for an agent, documents and sample questions.
