Frequently asked questions

How much does a presentation website cost?

From 120,000 ₽. The price depends on the number of pages, animation and 3D, languages and whether you need a catalog. We give the exact estimate after the first call, before work starts.

How long does it take?

From 2 weeks. We fix the timeline in the estimate together with the number of revision rounds.

Can we change texts and prices ourselves?

Yes. You can take the site with an editor (+30,000 ₽ one-off) or leave it with us on maintenance: you message us in Telegram and we make the change.

Will the site show up in search?

Basic search setup is part of the work: page titles and descriptions, a sitemap, markup for search engines, fast loading.

What do you need from us?

Access to the domain, plus texts and photos if you have them. We’ll gather the rest together on a call.

What if we already have a site?

We’ll do a redesign, from 85,000 ₽. We keep page addresses and search positions, and the old site keeps working while we build the new one.
