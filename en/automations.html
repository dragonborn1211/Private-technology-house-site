Frequently asked questions

Briefly about price, timelines and what stays with you.

Websites and apps

Who owns the site once it is paid for?

Yours. We hand over the code and access in full, and you decide where it lives and who runs it.

How much does it cost?

A website is from 120,000 ₽, a redesign from 85,000 ₽, an app from 150,000 ₽, server tasks from 5,000 ₽. Subscriptions: website support from 10,000 ₽ a month, automation and AI from 5,000 ₽ a month. We give the exact estimate and timeline after the first call, before work starts.

Where is the data stored?

We keep Russian projects on servers in Russia and international ones abroad. Forms, consents and cookies are set up under Russian law 152-FZ.

Do you work with Bitrix24 and 1C?

Bitrix24, yes: leads, deals, webhooks and the REST API. 1C data exchange we build together with your 1C specialist.

Can I see projects under NDA?

Yes. We show work under NDA on a call.

Subscription and templates

What do you need from us to connect?

Access to the services we connect: a Bitrix24 webhook, a Telegram bot, a marketplace seller API key. We set up the rest ourselves.

Where does it run?

On our server in Russia. We make sure the workflow keeps running and fix it if a service changes its API.

Can the template be adapted to us?

Yes. A template is a starting point: fields, messages and rules are tuned to your process.

What happens if I cancel?

The workflow stops with the subscription; we export your data on request.
