05 — Your night

How a night goes

Six steps from the car park to the last star. Each one leaves you something to keep.

  1. 01ArrivalCheck in from 16:00. The road is paved all the way to the door.→ keys and a red torch
  2. 02DinnerThree courses on the terrace while the sun goes down.→ sunset at the table
  3. 03BriefingTwenty minutes on what’s up tonight and how to look at it.→ your own star map
  4. 04The domeTwo to three hours at the eyepiece with our astronomer.→ a dozen objects, seen yourself
  5. 05Open skyThe terrace stays open: loungers, blankets and big binoculars.→ the Milky Way overhead
  6. 06MorningBreakfast until noon. Nobody will wake you.→ a photo of your night

06 — House rules

House rules

Not a list of amenities. Five rules that keep the sky dark and the nights good.

  1. 01Dark sky comes first.No white light outdoors after dusk, ever.
  2. 02Eight guests per astronomer.So everyone gets real time at the eyepiece.
  3. 03Clouds move your night, not your money.Rebook any date within 12 months, free.
  4. 04Quiet after ten.Low voices, soft doors, phones on night mode.
  5. 05The sky is the show.No lasers, no projections. Only what is really up there.
